Plenty of email clients ignore <style> tags. Juice moves your CSS into style attributes, so your design survives Gmail, Outlook and everything in between.

import juice from 'juice'

juice('<style>p { color: red }</style><p>Hi</p>')
// <p style="color: red;">Hi</p>
